    What should the AI do?
    Protect entries

    A protected entry keeps the lineup it has on DraftKings. Every other filled entry is open to review and replacement.

    Then start a chat with an AI, attach the document, and say "follow the instructions in this file." It will send back its proposal and, unless you chose Audit Only, a DraftKings upload file.

    What goes in the document

    Your contests and entries
    Read from the file you add. Nothing is uploaded; it stays on this device.
    Your lineups
    The best lineups you built in the Optimizer on this device, picked up automatically.
    The playbook and instructions
    What you want done (rebuild the portfolio, fill empty entries, or audit only), the rules the AI should follow, and anything you type in the box.
    What is on DraftKings now
    The lineups your entries already hold, and how much of your money rides on each player, pair and stack, so the AI can show before and after.
    The player pool
    Salaries, projections and your locks and exclusions, so the AI works from the same numbers you did.
