    Questions about rating a DraftKings lineup

    How do I get my DraftKings lineup rated?
    Take a screenshot of your lineup in DraftKings. Pick cash or tournament above, upload the screenshot, check the nine players we read from it, and tap the button. A small file downloads. Attach it to a new chat in Claude or ChatGPT and send "Rate my lineup." You can also skip the upload and send the screenshot to the AI along with the file.
    What do I get back?
    A letter grade, how many projected points your lineup sits behind the best one this week for that kind of contest, the one or two swaps that would help most, and what to check before lock.
    Why do I have to say cash or tournament?
    Because the same lineup can be good in one and poor in the other. Cash games reward the most dependable points. Tournaments reward lineups that can finish first, which means players who score together and some players the field is not using.
    Why does tournament size matter?
    In a small tournament a strong, sensible lineup can win. In a huge one, thousands of people play the popular players, so winning takes a higher ceiling and a lineup fewer people share.
    What is my lineup compared with?
    When you tap the button, this page builds the best lineups it can for your contest type from this week's projections. Those go in the file as the standard your lineup is measured against, along with every player's salary, projection and expected ownership.
    Is it free?
    Yes. No signup, no ads and nothing to buy. You only need an AI chat that accepts a file.
    Does the site see my screenshot?
    No. If you add it here, your own browser reads the player names from it on your device. The picture is never sent to this site, and only the nine player names go into your file.
    Which slates does it cover?
    The DraftKings NFL slate named at the top of this page. A lineup from a different slate cannot be rated, because the players and salaries would not match.
    Will it make me win?
    No one can promise that. It is a second opinion built on point projections, ownership estimates and a playbook. It does not know win odds and it can be wrong, so check injuries and contest rules yourself before lock.
