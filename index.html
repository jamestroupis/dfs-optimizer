    How this DFS optimizer works

    1. Set your player pool

    The page opens with this week's DraftKings NFL Classic salaries and a starting set of projections. Change any projection or ownership figure, lock players in, rule players out, or load your own projections CSV.

    2. Optimize lineups

    Choose cash or tournament, pick a quarterback stack and bring-back, and set exposure limits if you want them. The optimizer searches every legal roster under the $50,000 cap and returns the highest-projected lineups your rules allow, up to 150.

    3. Set lineups into your entries

    Load your DraftKings Edit Entries CSV, assign lineups to contests, and download a file ready to upload back to DraftKings. Every export is checked for roster, salary and entry-ID errors first.

    Common questions

    Is my DraftKings file uploaded anywhere?
    No. The optimizer runs on your device. Your entries, projections and lineups stay in your browser.
    Which contests does it support?
    DraftKings NFL Classic: one quarterback, two running backs, three wide receivers, one tight end, one flex and one defense under a $50,000 salary cap.
    How does it choose lineups?
    It ranks complete nine-player lineups by total projected points, less any ownership lean you set. It does not estimate floors, ceilings or win odds, because point projections alone do not support them.
    Does it handle late swap?
    It places the latest kickoff in the flex spot so a swap stays open. It does not yet re-optimize lineups after games have started.
