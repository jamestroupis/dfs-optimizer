How this DFS optimizer works

Set your player pool

The page opens with this week's DraftKings NFL Classic salaries and a starting set of projections. Change any projection or ownership figure, lock players in, rule players out, or load your own projections CSV.

Choose your rules

Pick cash or tournament, a quarterback stack from QB + 1 to QB + 4, bring-backs for game stacks, an ownership lean, and exposure limits if you want them. Nothing is limited until you say so.

Build and compare lineups

The optimizer searches every legal roster under the $50,000 cap and returns the highest-projected lineups your rules allow, up to 150, with exposure by player, quarterback and game.

Common questions

Is anything uploaded?
No. The optimizer runs on your device. Your projections and lineups stay in your browser.
Which contests does it support?
DraftKings NFL Classic: one quarterback, two running backs, three wide receivers, one tight end, one flex and one defense under a $50,000 salary cap.
How does it choose lineups?
It ranks complete nine-player lineups by total projected points, less any ownership lean you set. It does not estimate floors, ceilings or win odds, because point projections alone do not support them.
How do I get lineups into my DraftKings contests?
Use Lineup Setup. Add your DraftKings entries file and it combines your contests, the lineups you built here, and a playbook of instructions into one document to hand to an AI, which sends back your upload file.
Backup slate file (site owner)

Both pages read the slate from the Google Sheet on every visit, so updating the sheet updates the site. slate.json is only the backup shown when the sheet cannot be reached. To refresh the backup, download it here and replace that file on the site.
